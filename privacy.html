What I keep

When you talk with me or use my services, I keep:

  • Your name and your business name.
  • Your USDOT number.
  • Your phone number and email.
  • Notes from our conversations.
  • Public records about your company from FMCSA, such as your company snapshot on FMCSA's public SAFER site.

If I called you first, your number came from your company's public FMCSA record.

During a Driver File Audit, your driver files stay with you. I look at them with you and don't take copies. My notes list drivers by number only (Driver 1, Driver 2), with no names, license numbers, or test results. For drug and alcohol test records, you tell me only "on file" or "not on file," and I never see a test result.

What I never collect

  • Social Security numbers.
  • Passwords or Login.gov sign-ins.
  • Card numbers. If you pay by card, Stripe handles the payment, and I never see your full card number.
  • Your drivers' license numbers, medical details, or drug and alcohol test results.

How I use it

  • To do the work you asked for.
  • To follow up with you about it.

I never sell or rent your information. I never share your mobile number, or your permission for me to text you, with anyone for their marketing.

Your information sits in the tools I use to run the business: email, phone, payments, and software that helps me keep notes and write messages, including AI tools. I don't share it with anyone else unless you ask me to or a court orders it.

This website

This site sets no cookies and doesn't track you. It has no ads and no forms.

I'll do what you ask and tell you when it's done. I keep payment records for taxes, even after I delete the rest.
